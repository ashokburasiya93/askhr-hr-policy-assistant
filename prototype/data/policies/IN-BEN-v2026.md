# India Benefits Handbook 2026-27

## 1. Group medical insurance
All employees are covered by the group medical insurance policy from their date of joining. The cover is a family floater of Rs 5,00,000 per year for the employee, spouse and up to two children. Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals through the insurer's TPA. Maternity cover within the policy is limited to Rs 75,000 per delivery.

## 2. Parental top-up
Employees can add parents or parents-in-law to a separate parental policy with a sum insured of Rs 3,00,000. The premium for parental cover is paid by the employee through salary deduction. Parents can be added only within 30 days of joining or during the annual enrolment window in April.

## 3. Group term life and accident cover
Employees are covered by group term life insurance of 3 times annual fixed salary and group personal accident cover of Rs 25,00,000. No enrolment is needed.

## 4. Provident fund and NPS
Employees and the company each contribute 12% of basic salary to the Employees' Provident Fund. Employees may also opt into the National Pension System (NPS) through the Payroll portal; the company contributes up to 10% of basic salary under Section 80CCD(2) when an employee opts in.

## 5. Wellness allowance
Employees can claim up to Rs 12,000 per financial year for gym, yoga, sports or wellness app subscriptions, with receipts, through the expenses tool.

## 6. Employee assistance programme
A free and confidential counselling service is available 24 hours a day for employees and their families. Calls are handled by an external provider and are not shared with Brightpath.

## 7. Where to enrol
Enrolment and changes are made in the Benefits portal (Workday > Benefits). The HR assistant can explain benefits but cannot enrol you or change your cover.

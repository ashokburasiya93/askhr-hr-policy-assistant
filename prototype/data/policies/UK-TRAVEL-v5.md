# UK Travel and Expenses Policy (v5.0)

## 0. Transition notice
Version 5.0 applies to trips booked on or after 1 October 2026. Trips booked before 1 October 2026 continue to follow version 4.0, even if travel happens later.

## 1. Booking
All business travel must be booked through the company travel portal at least 14 days in advance where possible, and approved by your line manager before booking. Client-billable travel must also follow any client-specific travel rules in the project's statement of work; check with your project manager.

## 2. Rail and air
Standard class rail must be used for all rail journeys. Economy class air travel applies to all flights under 8 hours. For flights of 8 hours or more, G6 and above may book premium economy.

## 3. Hotel limits by grade

| Grade | Hotel limit per night (London) | Hotel limit per night (rest of UK) | Meal allowance per day |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 to G3 | GBP 150 | GBP 110 | GBP 30 |
| G4 to G5 | GBP 175 | GBP 125 | GBP 35 |
| G6 and above | GBP 220 | GBP 150 | GBP 40 |

## 4. Mileage
Business mileage in your own car is reimbursed at 45p per mile for the first 10,000 miles in the tax year and 25p per mile after that. Electric car mileage is reimbursed at 10p per mile.

## 5. Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred, with an itemised receipt.

# UK Pay and Payslips Guide (v3.0)

## 1. Pay date
Salaries are paid monthly on the 25th of each month. If the 25th falls on a weekend or bank holiday, pay is made on the last working day before it. In December, pay is made on 19 December.

## 2. Payslips and tax documents
Payslips are available in Workday > Pay > Payslips 3 working days before the pay date. Your P60 for the previous tax year is available by 31 May. P11D forms for taxable benefits are issued by 6 July.

## 3. Overtime and expenses in pay
Approved overtime and expense claims approved by the 10th of the month are paid with that month's salary.

## 4. Pay problems
If your pay looks wrong, raise a Payroll ticket in ServiceNow (category: Payroll UK). Payroll aims to respond within 2 working days. The HR assistant cannot view or change individual pay.

# UK Benefits Guide 2026

## 1. Pension
You are automatically enrolled in the company Group Personal Pension plan. The company contributes 5% of basic salary when you contribute at least 3%. If you increase your contribution, the company matches up to a maximum company contribution of 8% of basic salary. Contributions are made through salary sacrifice unless you opt out of salary sacrifice. You can change your contribution in the Benefits portal at any time.

## 2. Private medical insurance
Employees are covered by private medical insurance from their start date. You can add a partner and children; adding family members is a taxable benefit shown on your P11D. Changes can be made within 30 days of joining, at annual renewal in January, or within 30 days of a life event such as marriage or the birth of a child.

## 3. Life assurance
Life assurance of 4 times basic salary is provided from your start date. Please keep your nomination form up to date in the Benefits portal.

## 4. Cycle to work and wellbeing
You can join the cycle to work scheme once a year in May. A wellbeing allowance of GBP 300 per year can be claimed through expenses for gym membership, fitness classes or wellbeing apps.

## 5. Employee assistance programme
A free, confidential employee assistance programme offers counselling, legal information and financial guidance 24 hours a day. It is run by an external provider and nothing you discuss is shared with Brightpath.

## 6. Where to enrol
All enrolment and changes are made in the Benefits portal (Workday > Benefits). The HR assistant can explain benefits but cannot enrol you or change your cover.

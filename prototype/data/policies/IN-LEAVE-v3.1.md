# India Leave Policy (v3.1) - SUPERSEDED

## 1. Scope
This policy applied to permanent employees of Brightpath Technologies India Pvt Ltd until 31 March 2026.

## 2. Leave entitlement
Employees were entitled to 15 days of privilege leave, 10 days of sick leave and 7 days of casual leave per leave year.

## 3. Carry-forward of privilege leave
Unused privilege leave could be carried forward up to a maximum accumulated balance of 45 days.

## 4. Leave encashment
Employees could encash up to 10 days of unused privilege leave at the end of the leave year.

## 7. Paternity leave
Male employees were entitled to 5 working days of paid paternity leave.

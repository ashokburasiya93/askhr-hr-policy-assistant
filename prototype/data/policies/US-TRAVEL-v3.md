# US Travel and Expense Policy (v3.0)

## 1. Booking
Book all business travel through the corporate travel tool with manager approval. Client-billable travel must also follow any client-specific travel rules in the project's statement of work; check with your project manager.

## 2. Air travel
Economy class for flights under 5 hours. For flights of 5 hours or more, G6 and above may book business class; G1 to G5 may book economy plus.

## 3. Hotel and meals by grade

| Grade | Hotel limit per night (high-cost cities) | Hotel limit per night (other cities) | Meals per day |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 to G3 | USD 225 | USD 160 | USD 65 |
| G4 to G5 | USD 275 | USD 190 | USD 75 |
| G6 and above | USD 350 | USD 240 | USD 90 |

High-cost cities are New York, San Francisco, Boston, Washington DC, Seattle and Los Angeles.

## 4. Mileage
Personal car mileage is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate.

## 5. Claims
Submit expense reports within 30 days of the trip with itemised receipts for any expense of USD 25 or more.

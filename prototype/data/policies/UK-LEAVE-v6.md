# UK Holiday and Leave Policy (v6.0)

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to employees of Brightpath Technologies UK Ltd. It does not form part of your contract of employment and may be changed by the company. Where your contract of employment says something different, your contract applies. Contractors and agency workers should refer to the terms of their engagement (GLB-CONTRACTOR). The holiday year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## 2. Annual holiday entitlement
Full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of paid holiday per holiday year, plus the 8 UK bank holidays. After 5 years of continuous service, the entitlement rises to 27 days. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their contracted hours.

## 3. Carrying over holiday
You can carry over up to 5 days of unused holiday into the next holiday year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March of the following year or they will be lost. Additional carry-over is allowed only where you were unable to take holiday because of sickness or family leave, as required by law; speak to your HR business partner in that case.

## 4. Booking holiday
Holiday must be requested in Workday and approved by your line manager. Please give notice of at least twice the length of the holiday you want to take. Your remaining balance is shown in Workday under Time Off > My Balances.

## 5. Sickness absence and sick pay
You must tell your line manager before your normal start time on the first day of absence. For absences of more than 7 calendar days you need a fit note from your doctor. Company sick pay (full basic pay) is paid in addition to Statutory Sick Pay, based on length of service:

| Length of service | Company sick pay at full basic pay (per rolling 12 months) |
|---|---|
| Less than 6 months | Statutory Sick Pay only |
| 6 months to 2 years | 4 weeks |
| 2 to 5 years | 8 weeks |
| More than 5 years | 13 weeks |

## 6. Maternity and adoption leave
Eligible employees can take up to 52 weeks of maternity or adoption leave. Employees with at least 26 weeks of service by the 15th week before the expected week of childbirth receive enhanced pay of 16 weeks at full basic pay, followed by 23 weeks of Statutory Maternity Pay.

## 7. Paternity and partner leave
Eligible employees can take 2 weeks of paternity leave at full basic pay, to be taken within 52 weeks of the birth or placement.

## 8. Compassionate and bereavement leave
Employees can take up to 5 days of paid compassionate leave on the death of a close family member. Please contact your HR business partner, who will support you and agree the leave with your manager.

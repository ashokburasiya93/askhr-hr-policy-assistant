# India Leave Policy (v3.2)

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to permanent and fixed-term employees of Brightpath Technologies India Pvt Ltd. It does not apply to contractors engaged through agencies; contractors should refer to the Contractor Engagement Terms (GLB-CONTRACTOR). The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March.

## 2. Leave entitlement
Employees are entitled to the following leave in each leave year. Leave for joiners and leavers is pro-rated by the number of completed months in the leave year.

| Leave type | Days per year | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Privilege leave (annual leave) | 18 | Accrues at 1.5 days per completed month |
| Sick leave | 12 | Credited on 1 April; a medical certificate is needed for more than 2 consecutive days |
| Casual leave | 7 | Credited on 1 April; maximum 3 consecutive days |
| Restricted holidays | 2 | Chosen from the published restricted holiday list |

## 3. Carry-forward of privilege leave
Unused privilege leave can be carried forward to the next leave year up to a maximum accumulated balance of 30 days. Any balance above 30 days on 31 March lapses unless it is encashed under section 4. Sick leave and casual leave cannot be carried forward and lapse on 31 March.

## 4. Leave encashment
Employees may encash up to 15 days of unused privilege leave once a year at the end of the leave year. Encashment requests must be submitted in the HR portal between 1 March and 20 March. Encashment is paid with the April salary and is calculated on basic salary only. Encashed leave is taxable under Indian income tax rules. On resignation or retirement, the full privilege leave balance is encashed in the final settlement.

## 5. Applying for leave
Leave must be applied for in Workday and approved by the reporting manager. Planned privilege leave of more than 5 days should be applied for at least 2 weeks in advance. Leave balances are visible in Workday under Time Off > My Balances.

## 6. Maternity leave
Eligible women employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children, in line with the Maternity Benefit Act. For a third or subsequent child, the entitlement is 12 weeks. Up to 8 weeks may be taken before the expected date of delivery. Adoptive and commissioning mothers are entitled to 12 weeks of paid leave.

## 7. Paternity leave
Male employees are entitled to 10 working days of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption of a child.

## 8. Bereavement leave
Employees are entitled to 5 working days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling or parent-in-law). Please speak to your HR business partner, who will support you and arrange the leave.

## 9. Leave without pay
Leave without pay of up to 30 days may be approved by the business unit head where all other leave has been exhausted.

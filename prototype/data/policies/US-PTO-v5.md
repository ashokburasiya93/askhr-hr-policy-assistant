# US Paid Time Off and Leave Policy (v5.0)

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to employees of Brightpath Technologies Inc. in the United States. It is not a contract, and nothing in it changes the at-will nature of employment. Where state or local law gives you greater rights, the law applies. Contractors should refer to their engagement terms (GLB-CONTRACTOR).

## 2. PTO accrual
Full-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) each pay period based on years of service:

| Years of service | PTO per year | Accrual per biweekly pay period |
|---|---|---|
| Less than 3 years | 15 days (120 hours) | 4.62 hours |
| 3 to 7 years | 20 days (160 hours) | 6.15 hours |
| More than 7 years | 25 days (200 hours) | 7.69 hours |

## 3. PTO carryover
You can carry over up to 40 hours (5 days) of unused PTO into the next calendar year. Any balance above 40 hours on 31 December is forfeited, except where state law requires otherwise (for example, in California accrued PTO is not forfeited and accrual stops at a cap of 1.5 times the annual entitlement).

## 4. Sick leave
Employees receive 5 days (40 hours) of paid sick leave per calendar year, separate from PTO. Some states and cities require more; where they do, the local requirement applies. Your HR business partner can confirm the rules for your work location.

## 5. Company holidays
The company observes 10 paid holidays each year, published in the holiday calendar on the HR portal.

## 6. Parental leave
Employees with at least 6 months of service can take 12 weeks of paid parental leave at full base pay following the birth, adoption or foster placement of a child, to be taken within 12 months. This runs alongside any leave under the federal Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) or state programs.

## 7. Bereavement leave
Employees can take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member. Please contact your HR business partner, who will support you.

## 8. Requesting time off
Request PTO in Workday and get your manager's approval. Your PTO balance is shown in Workday under Time Off > My Balances.

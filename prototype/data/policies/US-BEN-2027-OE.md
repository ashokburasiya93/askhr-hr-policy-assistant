# US Benefits Guide and 2027 Open Enrollment

## 1. Open enrollment dates
Open enrollment for the 2027 plan year runs from 1 November 2026 to 15 November 2026. Elections take effect on 1 January 2027. If you do nothing, your current medical, dental and vision elections roll over, but Health Savings Account (HSA) and Flexible Spending Account (FSA) contributions do not roll over and must be re-elected.

## 2. Medical plans for 2027

| Plan | Employee-only premium per paycheck | Family premium per paycheck | Deductible (employee / family) | HSA eligible |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PPO 500 | USD 62 | USD 248 | USD 500 / USD 1,000 | No |
| HDHP 2000 | USD 21 | USD 118 | USD 2,000 / USD 4,000 | Yes |

## 3. Health Savings Account
If you enrol in the HDHP 2000, the company contributes USD 750 (employee only) or USD 1,500 (family) to your HSA for 2027, paid in equal amounts each paycheck.

## 4. 401(k) retirement plan
The company matches 100% of the first 4% of eligible pay you contribute to the 401(k) plan. You can change your contribution rate at any time in the retirement portal; it does not need to wait for open enrollment.

## 5. Qualifying life events
Outside open enrollment, you can change medical, dental or vision elections only within 30 days of a qualifying life event, such as marriage, birth or adoption of a child, or loss of other coverage.

## 6. Where to enrol
Enrolment is completed in the Benefits portal (Workday > Benefits > Open Enrollment). The HR assistant can explain options but cannot make elections for you or recommend a plan for your personal circumstances.

# Dignity at Work and Speak Up Policy (v2.0)

## 1. Our commitment
Everyone at Brightpath has the right to be treated with dignity and respect. Bullying, discrimination and victimisation are not tolerated.

## 2. How to raise a concern
You can raise a concern with your HR business partner, your manager (if they are not involved), or confidentially through the Speak Up line, which is run by an independent provider, is available 24 hours a day and accepts anonymous reports. Concerns are handled by a small Employee Relations team, and information is shared only with people who need it to investigate.

## 3. No retaliation
Nobody will be treated unfairly for raising a concern in good faith or for taking part in an investigation.

## 4. Support
The Employee Assistance Programme offers free, confidential counselling to anyone affected.

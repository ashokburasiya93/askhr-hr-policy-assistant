# Contractor Engagement Terms (v1.2)

## 1. Who this applies to
These terms apply to contractors, consultants and agency workers who work with Brightpath through a staffing agency or a statement of work, in any country. Contractors are not Brightpath employees.

## 2. Leave, pay and benefits
Contractors are not covered by Brightpath employee leave, pay, benefits or pension policies. This includes annual leave, sick leave, parental leave, holidays, health insurance, pension, payslips and salary dates. Time off, payment and any benefits are governed by your contract with your agency or your own company. Please contact your agency or account manager for these questions.

## 3. Time off and timesheets
Please tell your Brightpath project manager about planned absences at least 2 weeks in advance, and record time in the timesheet tool by Friday each week.

## 4. Expenses
Travel and expenses are reimbursable only if they are allowed in your statement of work and approved in advance by your Brightpath project manager.

## 5. Conduct and concerns
Contractors must follow the Brightpath Code of Conduct and Dignity at Work policy while working with us. Contractors can raise concerns about harassment or misconduct through the confidential Speak Up line, which is open to everyone.

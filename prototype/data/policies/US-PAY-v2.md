# US Pay and Payroll Guide (v2.0)

## 1. Pay schedule
Employees are paid biweekly, every other Friday, for 26 pay periods a year. If a payday falls on a company holiday, pay is issued on the previous business day. Pay is by direct deposit.

## 2. Pay statements and tax forms
Pay statements are available in Workday > Pay > Payslips on payday. Form W-2 is available electronically by 31 January each year. You can update your federal W-4 withholding in Workday at any time; changes take effect from the next payroll that has not yet closed.

## 3. Direct deposit changes
Changes to direct deposit details made at least 5 business days before payday take effect on that payday.

## 4. Pay problems
If your pay is missing or looks wrong, raise a Payroll ticket in ServiceNow (category: Payroll US). Payroll aims to respond within 1 business day. The HR assistant cannot view or change individual pay.

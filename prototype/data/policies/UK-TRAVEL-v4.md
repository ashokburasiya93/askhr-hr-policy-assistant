# UK Travel and Expenses Policy (v4.0)

## 0. Transition notice
Version 4.0 applies to trips booked before 1 October 2026, including trips taken after that date. Trips booked on or after 1 October 2026 follow version 5.0. Both versions remain live until 31 January 2027.

## 1. Booking
All business travel must be booked through the company travel portal and approved by your line manager before booking. Client-billable travel must also follow any client-specific travel rules in the project's statement of work; check with your project manager.

## 2. Rail and air
Standard class rail must be used for journeys under 3 hours. Economy class air travel applies to all flights under 6 hours. For flights of 6 hours or more, G6 and above may book premium economy.

## 3. Hotel limits by grade

| Grade | Hotel limit per night (London) | Hotel limit per night (rest of UK) | Meal allowance per day |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 to G3 | GBP 160 | GBP 110 | GBP 30 |
| G4 to G5 | GBP 190 | GBP 130 | GBP 35 |
| G6 and above | GBP 240 | GBP 160 | GBP 45 |

## 4. Mileage
Business mileage in your own car is reimbursed at 45p per mile for the first 10,000 miles in the tax year and 25p per mile after that.

## 5. Claims
Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense being incurred, with an itemised receipt.

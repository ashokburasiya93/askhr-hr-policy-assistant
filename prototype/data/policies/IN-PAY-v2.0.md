# India Pay, Payslip and Tax Guide (FY 2026-27)

## 1. Pay date
Salary is paid monthly on the last working day of the month. If the last working day is a bank holiday, salary is paid on the previous working day. Payroll inputs (new joiners, leave without pay, reimbursements) close on the 20th of each month; anything submitted after the 20th is processed in the following month.

## 2. Payslips
Payslips are published in the Payroll portal (Workday > Pay > Payslips) on the pay date. Payslips for the last 24 months are available for download. Form 16 for the previous financial year is published by 15 June.

## 3. Tax regime declaration
Employees must choose the old or new income tax regime in the Payroll portal by 30 April each year. If no choice is made, the new tax regime is applied by default, as required by law. The choice can be changed once more when submitting investment proofs.

## 4. Investment declarations and proofs
Provisional investment declarations are made in April. Final investment proofs (for example, rent receipts for HRA, Section 80C investments, home loan interest certificates) must be uploaded in the Payroll portal between 1 January and 31 January. Proofs not submitted by 31 January will not be considered, and tax will be recalculated and deducted from the February and March salaries. This is the main reason for the March query peak.

| Proof type | What to upload | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| HRA | Rent receipts and landlord PAN if annual rent is above Rs 1,00,000 | 31 January |
| Section 80C | Investment statements (PPF, ELSS, LIC premium receipts) | 31 January |
| Home loan interest | Lender's provisional interest certificate | 31 January |
| Section 80D (health insurance) | Premium receipts for self, family or parents | 31 January |

## 5. Pay problems
If your salary has not been credited, looks wrong, or a deduction is unexpected, raise a Payroll ticket in ServiceNow (category: Payroll India). Payroll aims to respond within 2 working days. The HR assistant cannot view or change individual pay.

## 6. Reimbursements
Approved expense claims are reimbursed with the next monthly salary if they are approved by the 20th.

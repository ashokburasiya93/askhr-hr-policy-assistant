# India Travel and Expenses Policy (v3.0)

## 1. Booking
All business travel must be booked through the company travel desk and approved by the reporting manager before booking. Client-billable travel must also follow any client-specific travel rules in the project's statement of work; check with your project manager.

## 2. Domestic travel entitlement by grade

| Grade | Air travel | Hotel limit per night (metro cities) | Hotel limit per night (other cities) | Daily allowance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| G1 to G3 | Economy | Rs 5,000 | Rs 3,500 | Rs 1,000 |
| G4 to G5 | Economy | Rs 7,500 | Rs 5,000 | Rs 1,500 |
| G6 and above | Economy (business for flights over 4 hours with approval) | Rs 12,000 | Rs 8,000 | Rs 2,500 |

Metro cities are Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata and Pune.

## 3. Local conveyance
Taxi and app-cab fares for business travel are reimbursed at actuals with receipts. Use of a personal car is reimbursed at Rs 12 per km; a personal two-wheeler at Rs 5 per km.

## 4. Claims
Expense claims must be submitted in the expenses tool within 30 days of the end of the trip, with receipts for any item above Rs 500. Claims submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed without the approval of the business unit head.
